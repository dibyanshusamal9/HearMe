Cost and business model

No new hardware. About ₹0 a flight to run.

Everything runs on devices already on board, so an airline pays one flat fee per aircraft: not per passenger, not per message.

What an airline needs

Passengers’ own phones They scan the QR code on the seat. No app to install.
The crew tablet already on board It runs the speech AI, the matching and the request list, with no internet.
Printed seat cards A QR code plus a picture card that works with no phone at all.
Nothing is wired into the aircraft, so there is no new equipment to certify. The airline’s IT team still approves the tablet app.
